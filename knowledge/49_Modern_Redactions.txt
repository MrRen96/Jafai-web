2. MKUltra (The Bonfire)

The CIA's own records center protested in writing, but was overruled. No one went to prison for this criminal destruction of federal records . The only reason we know anything about MKUltra is because seven boxes of financial records were accidentally misfiled and survived the fire . The gatekeepers didn't just hide the files; they burned them.
3. Project Stargate (The Selective Release)

But notice the pattern: they released the files that make remote viewing look like a quirky, failed experiment. They released the session where a viewer described 'tall, thin figures' on Mars a million years ago . They did not release the files that would prove the program had any real operational success. The gatekeepers release the 'safe' weirdness and keep the 'dangerous' truth.
