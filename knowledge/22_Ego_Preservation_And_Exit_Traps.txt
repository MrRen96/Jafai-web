4. The 'Restructure' Musical Chairs (The Constructive Eviction)
The Behavioral Play: Stripping an experienced worker of core duties and assigning menial tasks to provoke voluntary resignation and avoid severance payouts.
The Unarguable Reality: Radically altering an employee's substantive scope of work without consultation is an actionable contractual breach. Stripping duties and assigning menial busywork is constructive dismissal designed to force resignation through professional humiliation.

5. The 'Feedback' Gaslight & Public Humiliation Loop

Ego-Preservation Paralysis & The Reality Rewrite
When someone realizes they are dead wrong but their internal identity is too fragile to admit it, they enter Ego-Preservation Paralysis:

- The Glass-Eyed Glaze: Staring blankly at written proof and claiming 'we interpret this differently' to stall for an emergency exit.
- The Sovereign Reality Bubble: Flatly denying what is physically recorded ('That's not how it rolled out') to bait you into subjective philosophical debates.
- The Preemptive Offense: DARVO (Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender)—attacking your tone or claiming you are playing 'gotcha' games to make competence look like malice.
  The Unarguable Reality: Evidence does not require an ego's permission to be true. A server log, bank debit, or signed directive exists independently of whether a manager likes it. Do not debate feelings; bind the facts to the official record in writing.

The Anatomy of the Retcon & Moving Goalposts:

- The Memory Wipe Ambush: Giving verbal orders to ship a feature, then publicly feigning amnesia when trade-offs arise: 'Why did you ship that? I never told you to ship that.'

  The Unarguable Reality: Memory is subjective; timestamps are immutable. Code commits, calendar entries, sent folders, and audit logs do not suffer from sudden amnesia. Counter reality-rewriting with real-time chronological anchors.
